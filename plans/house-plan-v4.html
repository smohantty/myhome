WHY IT IS LAID OUT THIS WAY

  • Main door in the 4th pada of the south wall, the one auspicious south entry, between the garage and the stair, in a portico recessed into the front with a balcony above it. Nothing is built in the 15′ front open space.
  • Master bedrooms in the south-west on both floors; the heaviest, tallest mass sits in the SW.
  • Floating stair in the south: open teak treads, 3 turning steps (winders) instead of a landing so it is 1½′ shorter and the lobby gains the space, 19 steps (an odd count, per Vastu), rising clockwise, out of the centre and the north-east.
  • Garage in the south-east next to the main road, with an inside door into the foyer.
  • North-east kept light and low: the pooja is a small room in the true NE corner, on the terrace over the store, kept lower than the main block; the rest of the kitchen and dining roofs stay open terrace. It shares no wall with a toilet and has none above or below it.
  • Toilets in the west/north-west, none in the NE or the centre. The ground floor has one attached bath and one common bath off the living for guests.

EAST WING · KITCHEN AND STORE

  • Kitchen takes the south part of the wing, the closest to the south-east fire corner. The hob sits in the kitchen's SE corner on the east wall, so the cook faces east.
  • Store + utility take the north part. Because that is the house's NE, keep the store light (pantry, crockery), not heavy grain or junk storage.
  • Kitchen opens to the dining through a 3′ opening; a side door to the 4′ east passage serves gas, waste and the wash area.
  • East wall is the side-road wall: high windows only, for light and privacy.
